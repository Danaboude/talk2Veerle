The newsletter in your dashboard is ready, and I've updated your guide to explain how it works. The updated guide is attached: **Handleiding-website-talk2-Veerle.docx**.

**What's new in the Nieuwsbrief tab:**
- Choose from 5 ready-made styles (Klassiek, Modern, Warm, Minimaal, Elegant) and change the colours to match your brand.
- Build your mail from blocks: titles, text, photos, buttons, quotes. Photos can be round, rounded or square, with a shadow or border, and you can set their width and height.
- A live preview next to the editor shows exactly how the mail will look, on a computer and on a phone.
- Your mails are saved automatically. You can start a new one at any time, or open an earlier mail, edit it and send it again.
- A history shows which mail went to whom.
- A daily limit protects your sending account: 90 newsletters per day. If you pick more people, the rest wait until the next day and you send them with one click on "Verstuur rest".
- Every mail has an "Uitschrijven" link at the bottom.
